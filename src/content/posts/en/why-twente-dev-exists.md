If you write software in Twente, you know the pattern. There is a meetup, but you only hear
about it after it happened. There is a company twenty minutes away doing exactly the kind of
work you find interesting, but you never find out. There is a job that fits perfectly, posted
only to a LinkedIn feed you did not open that week.

The problem is not that too little happens here. The problem is that it is scattered.

## What this is

One place to see what is going on: upcoming meetups, open positions at real employers from
the region, and which companies here actually build software, and with what. Bilingual,
because the Twente developer population is bilingual — a substantial share of the people
working here arrived via the University of Twente and do not speak Dutch.

## What this is not

Not a job board full of recruiter reposts. Not a newsletter that tells you every week that
nothing happened. Not a platform that sells your data — there is not a single cookie on this
site, and there will not be.

## How it works

Everything on this site lives in a git repository. Adding a meetup is a pull request. Posting
a job is a pull request. That sounds like a lot of friction if you are not used to it, and a
plain form is coming for exactly that reason. But the underlying data stays open and
auditable, and that is precisely what separates a community site from a platform.
