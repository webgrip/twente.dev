What should Twente know you are building, or learning the hard way?

On Wednesday 4 November, [twente.dev/001: Reconnect](/en/001) opens with two half-hour **talks**:
candid stories from people who did the work. Not keynotes, not product demos, but one claim,
one real example, and one question for the room.

## What we are looking for

Send us four things, each a sentence or two:

1. **One claim**: a precise statement, not a topic label. "Our retries made the outage worse"
   beats "Lessons about resilience".
2. **One real example**: the decision, failure, demonstration or before/after result that makes
   the claim testable.
3. **What changed**: the practice you kept.
4. **One question for the room**: the unresolved part others can help with.

Software, hardware, embedded, manufacturing, data, security, design, product, research,
education: every discipline is welcome, and cross-overs are the point of the evening.

## What you get

You do not need speaking experience. Accepted contributions are shaped together: an editorial
call, a slide template, a technical rehearsal, a visible time cue on the night, and travel costs
agreed in advance. The room is deliberately small; the current capacity is on the
[release page](/en/001). The format is designed to be kind to first-time speakers.

## What we do not accept

Product pitches, paid speaking, or confidential material you are not authorised to share.
Speakers are selected editorially: [no one can buy stage time](/en/partners).

## How to submit

Email your four sentences to [hello@twente.dev](mailto:hello@twente.dev). We reply to every
submission, and decline with a reason within a week. Not ready for a stage? There are
[plenty of other ways to contribute](/en/contribute).
