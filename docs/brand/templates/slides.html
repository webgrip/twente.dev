why this exists

The region ships more than it shows

What we saw. Meetups faded after 2020 and never restarted. The people are still here — at the UT, at the corporates, in the startups — but the thread between them is gone.

What we do about it. A recurring evening, a site that lists every community, and field notes from people who run things in production. We build it. We run it. We share it.
